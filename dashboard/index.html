How the numbers work, and data checks
Cards
Yellow plus red cards shown in a match, both teams together.
Booking points
10 per yellow and 25 per red, the usual bookmaker convention.
Over 3.5 / 4.5 / 5.5
Share of a referee's matches with more cards than that line: over 4.5 means 5 or more.
Adjusted
Each match counts by its season: this season fully, last season 0.6, the one before 0.36. The result is then pulled toward the league average as if the referee had 8 extra average matches, so a few wild games can't top the table.
Projected cards
The referee's adjusted rate (or the league average if none is appointed), scaled by how card-heavy both teams' matches usually are.
Sources
Cards per match from football-data.co.uk, referees and fixtures from football-data.org. Referees for upcoming matches that football-data.org doesn't list yet are looked up on each league's official appointment pages when the dashboard is refreshed. The two are matched by league, date and teams. The current season plus the two before it.
Left out
Matches with no card data (an abandoned match that was awarded) and, for referee figures, matches with no referee name.
